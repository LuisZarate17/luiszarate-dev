Recommendation

Build Final Cut, and borrow one thing from each of the others

Final Cut matches the brief most directly: the frame is cinematic and quiet, and each wow moment is a small simulation of the project's real behaviour, which is the strongest proof of design engineering. It needs no WebGL to work, so it is safe on phones. Take Constellation's point field for the hero only, loaded after first paint, and Meridian's operable interface cards for the deeper technical layer.

A · Final CutWow from: runnable 2D simulations.Build: CSS, canvas 2D, no three.js.Main risk: scroll-pinned scenes on iOS Safari.
B · MeridianWow from: gradient and operable UI cards.Build: one small shader, mostly DOM.Main risk: reads as a Stripe imitation.
C · ConstellationWow from: one morphing 3D field.Build: three.js, shaders, tuning time.Main risk: phone GPUs, and shapes that do not read.
